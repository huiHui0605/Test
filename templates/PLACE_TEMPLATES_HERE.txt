Use Certificate Templates in the desktop app to select your approved PPTX files.
The original volunteer_template.pptx is included.
Clearly labeled demo templates are in ../examples/.
Use editable Name text for the recipient; the default name marker is Name.
Legacy {{NAME}} text is also supported.
Use Date No for attendance date and Cert No for certificate number.
Choose all three source columns above the Volunteer Certificates table.
Legacy markers: {{DATE}}, {{TYPE}}, {{ID}}; {{CERT_NO}} is also supported.
Do not put participant data or Google service-account credentials in this folder.
